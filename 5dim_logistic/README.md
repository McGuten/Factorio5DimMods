# 5Dim's Mod - Logistic

Make your robot network faster, wider and more capable.

5Dim Logistic adds more tiers of robots and roboports so construction, supply and large-area automation can scale much further than vanilla.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of construction robots.
- 10 tiers of logistic robots.
- 10 tiers of roboports.
- Stronger robot coverage and better logistics network growth.

## Good Fit If You Want

- Faster construction across large areas.
- Better robot-based malls and supply systems.
- A stronger late-game logistics network than vanilla offers.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Pairs especially well with 5Dim Storage and Transport.
- Great for players who lean heavily on bots and large build projects.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
