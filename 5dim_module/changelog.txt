Version: 2.1.1
Date: 2026-08-05
  Bugfixes:
    - Beacons had a wildly excessive number of module slots on the higher tiers. The MK10 came with
      82 of them and reached across most of a factory on its own.
  Changes:
    - Beacon tiers now grow at a steady pace. Every tier spreads module effects a little better,
      most tiers reach one tile further, and every second tier adds a module slot.
    - The MK10 beacon holds 6 modules, spreads their effect twice as well as the original beacon and
      reaches ten tiles out.
    - Higher tier beacons draw more power, in line with what they now give back.
