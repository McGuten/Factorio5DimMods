# 5Dim's Mod - Vehicles

Take combat and mobility further with stronger tanks and spidertrons.

5Dim Vehicles adds more tiers of tanks and spidertrons so mobile warfare, expansion and nest clearing remain strong deeper into the game.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of tanks.
- 10 tiers of spidertrons.
- Better survivability, power and equipment potential across vehicle progression.
- More room for vehicle-based combat through the late game.

## Good Fit If You Want

- Stronger frontline vehicles.
- Better mobile combat tools.
- More progression for players who enjoy clearing territory in person.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Works especially well with 5Dim Equipment and Battlefield.
- A strong choice for combat-focused runs and aggressive expansion.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
