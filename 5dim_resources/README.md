# 5Dim's Mod - Resources

Process ore faster and get more value out of every mining patch.

5Dim Resources expands smelting and ore processing with stronger furnaces, mashers and dust chains that reward larger industrial builds.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of electric furnaces.
- 10 tiers of mashers for ore grinding.
- Dust processing chains for improved material yield.
- Industrial furnace-style processing for larger smelting setups.
- More progression in the step between raw ore and finished plates.

## Good Fit If You Want

- Stronger smelting throughput.
- Extra depth in ore processing.
- More rewarding late-game production chains around metals and intermediates.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Especially useful if your base is bottlenecked by smelting and plate production.
- Works very well with 5Dim Mining, Transport and Automation.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
