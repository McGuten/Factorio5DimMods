# 5Dim's Mod - Space Age

Upgrade the full Space Age production chain, planet by planet.

5Dim Space Age extends planetary and orbital machinery across every Space Age location, so DLC progression keeps growing into the late game. Each planet gets more tiers of its signature buildings, with stronger materials and higher throughput as you climb the tier ladder.

## What This Mod Adds, By Planet

### Vulcanus

The volcanic foundry world. Extra tiers for its metallurgy and heavy mining chain:

- More tiers of foundries for molten metal casting.
- More tiers of big mining drills for large-scale ore extraction.

### Gleba

The organic, agricultural world. Extra tiers for its biological production chain:

- More tiers of biochambers for organic processing.
- More tiers of agricultural towers for crop farming.
- More tiers of captive biter spawners for biological resources.
- More tiers of biolabs for Gleba-based research.

### Fulgora

The ruined, lightning-powered world. Extra tiers for its electromagnetic and scrap chain:

- More tiers of electromagnetic plants for advanced electronics.
- More tiers of recyclers for scrap reprocessing.
- More tiers of lightning rods and lightning collectors for storm power.

### Aquilo

The frozen, late-game world. Extra tiers for its cryogenic and heat chain:

- More tiers of cryogenic plants for cold-temperature chemistry.
- More tiers of heating towers for keeping machinery running.

### Space platforms and orbit

The orbital logistics layer that connects every planet. Extra tiers for platform infrastructure:

- More tiers of cargo bays and cargo landing pads for orbital storage and delivery.
- More tiers of asteroid collectors and crushers for asteroid mining.
- More tiers of thrusters for faster platform travel.

### Cross-planet endgame tech

Late-game systems that span the whole Space Age progression:

- More tiers of fusion reactor buildings and fusion generators.
- More tiers of fusion equipment and mech armor for personal endgame loadouts.

## Good Fit If You Want

- A longer Space Age progression.
- Stronger late-game planet and platform production.
- More room to scale a full DLC factory beyond vanilla limits.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.
- Requires the Space Age DLC.
- Requires Quality support used by the module setup.

## Notes

- Adds a spoilage time multiplier setting (in 5dim_core) to tune how fast perishable items spoil.
- Designed for players who are fully committed to a Space Age run.
- One of the best late-game expansion modules in the 5Dim suite.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
