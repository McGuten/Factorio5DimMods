# 5Dim's Mod - Nuclear

Scale nuclear power far beyond vanilla.

5Dim Nuclear extends the full nuclear chain with stronger reactors, heat systems, turbines and centrifuges for much larger power blocks.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of nuclear reactors.
- 10 tiers of heat exchangers.
- 10 tiers of heat pipes.
- 10 tiers of steam turbines.
- 10 tiers of centrifuges.
- A longer progression for high-output power generation.

## Good Fit If You Want

- Bigger nuclear plants.
- More late-game power options.
- A stronger uranium-based energy path for large factories.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Pairs naturally with 5Dim Energy.
- Especially useful once your factory grows past what basic solar or steam setups can comfortably support.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
