# 5Dim's Mod - Space Age

Upgrade the full Space Age production chain.

5Dim Space Age extends planetary and orbital machinery across Vulcanus, Gleba, Fulgora, Aquilo and space platforms, so DLC progression keeps growing into the late game.

## What This Mod Adds

- More tiers for key Vulcanus machines such as foundries and big mining drills.
- More tiers for Gleba machines such as biochambers and agricultural towers.
- More tiers for Fulgora machines such as electromagnetic plants and recyclers.
- More tiers for Aquilo machines such as cryogenic plants and heating towers.
- More tiers for space platform infrastructure such as cargo bays, asteroid collectors, crushers and thrusters.

## Good Fit If You Want

- A longer Space Age progression.
- Stronger late-game planet and platform production.
- More room to scale a full DLC factory beyond vanilla limits.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.
- Requires the Space Age DLC.
- Requires Quality support used by the module setup.

## Notes

- Designed for players who are fully committed to a Space Age run.
- One of the best late-game expansion modules in the 5Dim suite.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
