# 5Dim's Mod - Enemies

Turn biters into a real long-term threat.

5Dim Enemies adds stronger enemy variants and heavier combat pressure across the game, making defense planning and military investment much more important.

## What This Mod Adds

- Multiple new biter variants with different strengths and resistances.
- Multiple new spitter variants with more dangerous attacks.
- Stronger enemy scaling for longer saves.
- A much harsher combat environment overall.

## Good Fit If You Want

- A more dangerous map.
- Heavier combat pressure through the whole game.
- A reason to invest in stronger defenses and military upgrades.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Mod settings let you toggle individual enemy variants and death effects.
- This module significantly increases difficulty.
- Recommended for experienced players, especially when paired with 5Dim Battlefield.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
