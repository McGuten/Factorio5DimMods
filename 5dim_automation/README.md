# 5Dim's Mod - Automation

Scale production without outgrowing your machines.

5Dim Automation extends your factory with more tiers of assemblers, chemical plants, refineries and labs, giving big production lines room to keep growing.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of assembling machines.
- 10 tiers of chemical plants.
- 10 tiers of oil refineries.
- 10 tiers of laboratories.
- A longer production progression for bigger factories and higher science demand.

## Good Fit If You Want

- More room to scale production without rebuilding everything around vanilla limits.
- Stronger production buildings for long saves and large bases.
- A smoother path from early automation to heavy late-game manufacturing.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Works especially well with 5Dim Module, Energy and Resources.
- Keeps the familiar production chain, but extends it through many more tiers.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
