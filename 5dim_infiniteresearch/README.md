# 5Dim's Mod - Infinite Research

Keep late-game science useful.

5Dim Infinite Research adds repeatable upgrades for production, mining, combat and logistics, giving large science bases a stronger long-term purpose.

## What This Mod Adds

- Infinite crafting speed research.
- Infinite mining speed research.
- Infinite bullet and laser damage research.
- Infinite worker robot upgrades.
- Infinite braking force and stack size improvements.
- More science sinks for post-rocket and long-term saves.

## Good Fit If You Want

- More late-game progression.
- Better rewards for giant science production.
- A stronger endgame loop after the standard tech tree is complete.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Best suited for long-running factories with strong science throughput.
- A natural fit alongside Automation, Module and Logistics-heavy setups.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
