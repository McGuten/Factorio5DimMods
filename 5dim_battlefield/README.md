# 5Dim's Mod - Battlefield

Build defenses that keep up with a more dangerous world.

5Dim Battlefield expands your military options with more tiers of turrets, walls, gates and radar support, giving your base a longer defensive progression than vanilla.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of gun turrets.
- 10 tiers of laser turrets.
- Sniper variants for both ballistic and laser defense.
- 10 tiers of tesla turrets and flamethrower turrets.
- 10 tiers of walls, gates and radars.
- A deeper defensive progression for longer saves.

## Good Fit If You Want

- Stronger perimeter defenses.
- More military progression through the mid and late game.
- Better tools to hold large bases against heavy attacks.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Pairs especially well with 5Dim Enemies if you want a tougher combat experience.
- Ideal for players who enjoy fortified bases and long defensive lines.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
