# 5Dim's Mod - Transport

Push more items and fluids through your factory.

5Dim Transport expands belts, inserters, loaders, pumps and pipe options so high-throughput factories can keep scaling without hitting vanilla transport limits too early.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of transport belts.
- 10 tiers of underground belts, including long-range variants.
- 10 tiers of splitters.
- Multiple loader tiers.
- 10 tiers of inserters across the main inserter families.
- 10 tiers of pumps and longer underground pipe options.

## Good Fit If You Want

- More throughput without replacing belts with bots too early.
- Stronger bus bases and high-volume factories.
- More room to scale liquids and item movement into the late game.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.
- Requires Bob's Inserters.
- Space Age integration is available when the DLC is installed.

## Notes

- Bob's Inserters is a hard requirement for this module.
