# 5Dim's Mod - Equipment

Upgrade your armor with stronger personal power, mobility and combat tools.

5Dim Equipment extends personal equipment progression with stronger batteries, movement, shields, reactors and combat systems for better late-game loadouts.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of batteries.
- 10 tiers of exoskeletons.
- 10 tiers of personal roboports.
- 10 tiers of personal laser defense and personal tesla defense.
- 10 tiers of portable solar panels, reactors and energy shields.
- Additional armor upgrades and stronger late-game personal setups.

## Good Fit If You Want

- Faster and more mobile personal movement.
- Better personal combat strength.
- Stronger builder and combat armor loadouts in the late game.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- A great companion module for Vehicles, Battlefield and Space Age saves.
- Especially useful for players who spend a lot of time fighting or building in person.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
