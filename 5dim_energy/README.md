# 5Dim's Mod - Energy

Expand your power grid from early boilers to late-game megabases.

5Dim Energy adds more tiers of generation, storage and power distribution, helping your factory stay supplied as demand keeps climbing.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of boilers and steam engines.
- 10 tiers of solar panels and accumulators.
- 10 tiers of medium poles, big poles and substations.
- 10 tiers of lamps.
- More headroom for growing factories with heavier power demand.

## Good Fit If You Want

- Fewer power bottlenecks as your base grows.
- Better tools for large electrical networks.
- More progression in power infrastructure beyond vanilla.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Pairs very well with 5Dim Automation, Nuclear and Space Age.
- Useful in both compact bases and large distributed power grids.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
