# 5Dim's Mod - Yeters

Push past the solar system edge with a new post-edge endgame planet.

5Dim Yeters adds Yeters, a harsh hybrid planet for Factorio: Space Age. It is unlocked after Promethium science, sits beyond the solar system edge and concentrates late Space Age resources on a single demanding surface, with its own discovery technology, a dedicated space route and Gleba-style agriculture.

## What This Mod Adds

- A new planet, Yeters, with a harsh hybrid surface (high pressure, high gravity, no day-night cycle and no entity freezing).
- A `yeters-discovery` technology gated behind the Promethium science pack and every other science pack.
- A new space connection from the solar system edge to Yeters.
- Dedicated Yeters soils that let yumako and jellynut grow on the planet.
- Gleba-style agriculture support so Space Age crops can be farmed on Yeters.
- A biochamber recipe (`yeters-spoilage`) that converts fresh yumako and jellynut into spoilage.
- A runtime system that pauses item spoilage on the Yeters surface, so perishable Space Age goods can be stored there.

## Why Go To Yeters

Yeters is a hybrid world that blends biomes from across the solar system, so a single surface gives you access to resources that normally require visiting several different planets:

- **Nauvis-style basics:** stone, coal, crude oil and uranium ore for the core production and power chains.
- **Vulcanus materials:** calcite, tungsten ore and sulfuric acid geysers for metallurgy and acid processing.
- **Fulgora salvage:** scrap fields for recycling into a wide spread of intermediate products.
- **Aquilo fluids:** lithium brine and fluorine vents for cryogenic and late-game chemistry.
- **Gleba biology:** yumako and jellynut farming on dedicated soils, plus iron and copper stromatolites.
- **Strong solar in orbit:** high space solar power above the planet for efficient platform energy.

Because so many resource chains overlap on one surface, Yeters lets you build a compact, mostly self-sufficient late-game base instead of splitting production across many planets.

## Good Fit If You Want

- A meaningful endgame goal after reaching the solar system edge.
- A single harsh planet that brings late Space Age resources together.
- A compact base that mixes Nauvis, Vulcanus, Fulgora, Aquilo and Gleba resource chains in one place.
- A practical way to store perishable Space Age items without losing them to spoilage.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.
- Requires the Space Age DLC.

## Notes

- Spoilage is only paused on the Yeters surface; spoilage on Nauvis, Gleba and other planets is unaffected.
- The spoilage pause preserves partial progress rather than resetting it.
- This is an early implementation focused on a playable planet, route and discovery. Future passes can add Yeters-specific machinery, compact hybrid assembly lanes and alternate local-fluid recipes.
