# 5Dim's Mod - Mining

Pull more resources out of the map with stronger extraction tools.

5Dim Mining adds more tiers of drills, pumpjacks and pumps so ore, oil and water extraction can keep pace with a much larger factory.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of electric mining drills.
- 10 tiers of pumpjacks.
- 10 tiers of water pumpjacks that can extract water on land.
- 10 tiers of offshore pumps.
- Stronger extraction tools for larger production chains.

## Good Fit If You Want

- More raw resource throughput.
- Better remote outposts and mining expansions.
- New options for solving water logistics in complex bases.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Water pumpjacks are one of the most practical changes in the module.
- Pairs well with 5Dim Resources, Transport and Trains.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
