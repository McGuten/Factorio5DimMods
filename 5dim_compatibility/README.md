# 5Dim's Mod - Compatibility

Keep mixed modpacks cleaner and easier to navigate.

5Dim Compatibility reorganizes supported third-party mod content into the 5Dim tab structure, making large mixed modpacks easier to browse and manage.

## What This Mod Adds

- Integration support for many popular external mods.
- Cleaner item organization when playing larger modpacks.
- Better consistency between 5Dim tabs and supported third-party content.
- A smoother browsing experience in crafting and inventory menus.

## Good Fit If You Want

- A cleaner interface in mixed modded saves.
- Better organization when using several big content mods together.
- The 5Dim tab structure to cover more than just the 5Dim modules.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.
- Works best when you also use supported third-party mods.

## Notes

- This module is about organization and integration, not about adding a new progression tree of its own.
- Especially useful in larger modpacks built around 5Dim plus other major mods.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
