# 5Dim's Mod - Quality

Extends the quality ladder up to 10 levels to match 5Dim's tiered progression.

5Dim Quality keeps the vanilla qualities and adds five more on top of Legendary, recolouring the whole ladder with the 5Dim tier palette so quality reads at a glance.

## What This Mod Adds

- Five extra qualities beyond Legendary: Mythic, Ascended, Divine, Cosmic and Eternal.
- A full 10-colour quality ladder aligned with the 5Dim tier colours.
- Recoloured, pip-style quality icons for every level.
- Default Factorio quality effect scaling for the new levels.

## Good Fit If You Want

- A quality progression that matches 5Dim's 10-tier machines.
- More end-game quality headroom for high quality modules and recyclers.

## Requirements

- 5dim_core
- Quality (Space Age quality system)
