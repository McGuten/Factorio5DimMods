# 5Dim's Mod - Trains

Move bigger loads faster with stronger rail logistics.

5Dim Trains adds more tiers of locomotives and wagons so rail networks can carry more cargo and fluids across larger factories.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of locomotives.
- 10 tiers of cargo wagons.
- 10 tiers of fluid wagons.
- More speed, hauling power and transport capacity for rail systems.

## Good Fit If You Want

- Better long-distance logistics.
- Larger train-based factories and outpost networks.
- More train progression than vanilla offers.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.

## Notes

- Works especially well with 5Dim Transport, Mining and Storage.
- A strong choice for rail-heavy and megabase-style saves.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
