# 5Dim's Mod - Storage

Store more items and fluids without constant rebuilds.

5Dim Storage adds larger tanks, chests and related storage options so big bases can buffer more materials with less layout churn.

## What This Mod Adds

- 10 tiers of storage tanks.
- 10 tiers of multi-connection storage tanks.
- 10 tiers of steel chests.
- 10 tiers of logistic chests when used with 5dim_logistic.
- More scalable buffering for item and fluid systems.

## Good Fit If You Want

- Bigger fluid buffers.
- Larger chest storage without swapping to modded warehouse-style systems.
- Cleaner large-scale logistics and mall storage.

## Requirements

- Requires 5dim_core.
- Logistic chest tiers become available when 5dim_logistic is installed.

## Notes

- A strong companion module for Logistic, Transport and Trains.
- The multi-connection tanks are especially useful in compact fluid layouts.

Part of the 5Dim mod collection for Factorio.
